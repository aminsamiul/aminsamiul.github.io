About & Competencies

Evidence-based planning meets spatial modeling

Transforming complex spatial, demographic, and environmental datasets into actionable urban infrastructure solutions.

I am Samiul Amin, an Urban and Regional Planning graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) with professional consultancy experience at Planning Design and Research Consultant (PDRC) in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My expertise spans the entire spatial analysis lifecycle — from designing enterprise geodatabases, topological QA/QC, and land use / land cover (LULC) remote sensing to supervising large-scale field enumerator teams across municipalities for LGED master planning projects.

Passionate about applying geospatial technologies (ArcGIS Pro, ArcMap, QGIS, Google Earth Engine) toward climate-responsive urban growth, thermal resilience, and sustainable municipal infrastructure.
